Official Biography and Profile of Aliyu Ibrahim (Yamagi)
About Aliyu Ibrahim
Aliyu Ibrahim (Yamagi) was born on 28 August 2000 in Kaduna State, Nigeria.
He obtained his National Diploma (ND) from Kaduna Polytechnic (Kadpoly).
Aliyu Ibrahim works in the field of metal construction and is actively involved in community and youth development.
Leadership
Aliyu Ibrahim is the current Youth Leader of the Old Panteka Industrial Development Association (OPIMA).
He assumed office as Youth Leader on 22 October 2023 and continues to serve in this position.
As Youth Leader, he represents young people within the association and contributes to activities that promote cooperation, development, and peaceful relationships among members.
Professional Background
Aliyu Ibrahim has experience in metal construction and continues to develop his professional skills while serving his community.
